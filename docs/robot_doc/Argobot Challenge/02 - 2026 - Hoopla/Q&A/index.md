## Day 1 Q&A Questions

---

Q: Is bulldozing a **HOOP** considered to be in control of the **HOOP**?

A: Bulldozing without clear intent to score is not considered a violation (I.e. you run into **HOOPS** on the ground while doing other things), but herding with the intent to score is considered a violation.

---

Q: What is the maximum speed the Argobot drivetrain can be set to?

A: The Argobot drivetrain must be limited to a maximum of 60% speed. Teams can still set their max speed threshold lower if they choose to.
