    Feedforward

    What these do

    Feedforward doesn't wait for error. kG holds the mechanism up against gravity. kS overcomes friction in the direction of the error, like Phoenix 6's closed-loop sign option. kV supplies the voltage a moving setpoint needs, so it only matters with the motion profile on.

    Feedback (PID)

    Advanced: kI and kD

    With good feedforward, many FRC mechanisms only need kP. kI can remove a small leftover error, but it builds up during long moves and causes overshoot. kD damps overshoot when kP alone rings.
